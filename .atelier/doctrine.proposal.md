# X4_TwitchMod: Doctrine

> **PROPOSED** by the Doctrine upkeep (r10) on 2026-10-12. It binds nothing until Simon
> approves it on the board's Doctrine page.

**D1. The streamer decides how much power chat has; chat decides what to do with it.**

**D2. When every choice carries its own cost, chat restrains itself and nobody has to police it.**

**D3. A viewer earns their say by being there, not by paying.**

**D4. Viewers can always see what they can do and what it costs.**

**D5. Viewers care most about what carries their name, so what they put their name on lasts.**

**D6. The streamer should never have to leave the game to play with chat.**
