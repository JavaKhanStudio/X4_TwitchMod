Envoy

M corvette, made by Argon and Teladi

A joint development by the Argon Federation and the Teladi Ministry of Finance, the Envoy integrates each faction's latest technologies. The Envoy class is equipped with exclusive high-performance engines and shield generators, unavailable to other vessels.

The joint technology program also included the development of a set of heavy weapons specific to the Envoy. Due to market demand, turret variants of those weapons were also introduced. These variants are compatible with a wide variety of ships, but some ships are excluded due to technical constraints. The ship also includes two forward-facing hardpoints, supporting a range of conventional weaponry for tactical flexibility.

A dedicated low-signature stance is available, in which most weapon systems are concealed and inoperable. While in this configuration, the vessel exhibits reduced detectability and increased manoeuvrability, at the expense of speed.

Ship operators may also activate faction camouflage protocols, allowing the vessel to appear to belong to another faction when observed externally. These advanced protocols are licensed on a strictly individual basis, and are not available to employed personnel.
