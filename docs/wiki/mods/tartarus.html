The Tartarus is a battleship of Paranid design. While it is faster and more nimble than other ships of its class its hull is also far weaker than that of other battleships. Instead the Tartarus favours heavy shielding, the heaviest of all battleships in fact. Even though it holds a good amount of large turrets it lacks somewhat in anti-fighter capabilities, instead relying on escorts.
