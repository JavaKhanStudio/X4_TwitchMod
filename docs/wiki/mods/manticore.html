The Manticore-class is the Argon response to the Split deploying the Caiman Heavy Frigate. It is itself a Heavy Frigate that trades large turrets capacity for a powerful destroyer-class main gun and improved manouverability. The Manticore is a ship well suited to all situations and capable of taking on ships much bigger than itself.
